Privacy Policy

Shadow AI Privacy Auditor — web app and Chrome extension. Last updated: 30 July 2026.

In one sentence: this product collects nothing, transmits nothing, and has no servers. Everything happens on your device, and it is technically prevented from making network requests.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no backend, no analytics, no accounts and no third-party services. The developer never receives your prompts, your findings, your settings, or any usage data.

Where your text is processed

All detection and redaction happens locally, inside your own browser tab (web app) or the extension's content script (extension). Your prompt text is never sent to a server, to the extension's background worker, or to the host page.

  • The web app ships a connect-src 'none' Content-Security-Policy, so the browser itself blocks any outbound request.
  • The extension's manifest sets connect-src 'none' and requests zero host permissions, so it cannot transmit data even if a bug tried to.
  • A continuous integration check (verify:offline) fails the build if any shipped file contains fetch, XMLHttpRequest, WebSocket, sendBeacon, a remote import, or a remote asset reference.

What is stored on your device

DataWhereContains
Your policy / settingschrome.storage.sync (extension) / localStorage (web app)Configuration only — enabled categories, thresholds, custom terms. Never prompt text.
Activity logchrome.storage.local (extension) / localStorage (web app)Counts only — risk scores and how many findings per category/severity. Never the text you scanned and never a detected value.
Onboarding flagchrome.storage.localA single boolean so the welcome message shows once.

This data never leaves your device and is never synced to us. You can clear it any time (extension options → Clear log, or your browser's site-data controls).

Permissions the extension requests

  • storage — to save your settings and the count-only activity log locally.
  • Content scripts on a fixed list of AI chat sites (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity and a few others) — to read the message box locally and warn you before you send. No host_permissions, no tabs, no scripting, no webRequest.

Children

The product is a general-purpose privacy tool and is not directed at children under 13.
